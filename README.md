Erin's Email explanantion. This email is to clarify the process for sending IRS Claim Summaries as part of our pro forma delivery process. I'm still working on the details of the how, but here is the why: 
Rule of thumb
For amended R&D tax credit claims for 2022 through 2024, we include the IRS Claim Summary with the pro forma delivery package.
The IRS Claim Summary should include:
The business components tied to the credit claim.
The research activities performed for each business component.
The QRE totals by category, including qualified wages, supplies, and contract research expenses.
When prepared to deliver, the attachment should be named Form6765ClaimInformation.pdf., and we should inform the Tax Preparer and client that they should submit it to the IRS the same way. 
The only exception is if the taxpayer chooses to include the information in Section G of the 6765, or if they are required and choose to (I use 'choose' because Section G is optional for everyone until 2026 Tax Year). 
Technical information behind the rule
This summary submission process is governed by the IRS rules for amended refund claims under IRC §41. The underlying legal standard comes from Treas. Reg. §301.6402-2(b)(1), which requires a refund claim to provide enough facts to apprise the IRS of the exact basis for the claim. The IRS applies that rule to amended R&D credit claims through its amended return FAQ, Form 6765 instructions, and IRM 4.46.3.7.6. The IRS states that amended research credit claims must identify the business components, identify the research activities performed for each business component, and provide total qualified wage, supply, and contract research expenses for the claim year.
Effective June 18, 2024, the IRS waived the requirement to include two individual level items at the time of filing: 1) The names or titles of the individuals who performed each research activity, or 2) The information each individual sought to discover. The IRS might still request that information later if the claim is selected for exam, but that is all data we maintain for them in our internal workpapers. 
The Form 6765 instructions state that if a taxpayer is claiming a refund or credit on an amended return or AAR involving a §41 credit that was either not reported on the original return or increased from the original return, the taxpayer must follow the required information procedures for a valid research credit claim. If e filed, the required claim information attachment should be named Form6765ClaimInformation.pdf. If a study or other documents are submitted, the exact pages containing the required information should be identified, and the attachment should be named Form6765Study.pdf.
The IRM provides us with the practical IRS review standard. It states that the research activity description must explain what the taxpayer did and how they did it by business component. It also states that the description doesnt need to walk through the four-part test in detail, but language that simply restates the Code or Treasury Regulations is insufficient (this is why we switched from the straight 'technique', 'process' labeling).
